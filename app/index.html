    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official descriptions. PowerPyx, which played the finished game, rates the set 4 out of 10 and estimates about 200 hours in a single playthrough — there is no mission replay, no New Game+, no difficulty setting and no online trophy. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy is flagged as potentially missable. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story.

      Save advice

      There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter — the game offers 15 manual save slots plus one autosave. Optional missions with black icons and pink letters can disappear after a while, but PowerPyx notes they do not matter for trophies: there are far more than the 100 the checklist asks for. Quest givers tied to trophies all return after the story.
